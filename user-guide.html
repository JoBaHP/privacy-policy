ServiceNow MSP Bridge

Installation & User Guide

Get your ServiceNow ITSM data into monday in a few minutes — see incidents, changes, problems and requests across every customer, act on them, and sync both ways. No ServiceNow seats for your monday users.

01What it does

ServiceNow MSP Bridge is a monday board view that connects to your ServiceNow instance and rolls up ITSM health per customer domain: open incidents, changes, problems, requests, SLA breaches and MTTR. You can drill into any customer, act on records (change state, add work notes, create new ones) straight from monday, and sync records onto monday boards with edits flowing both ways.

02Prerequisites

  • A ServiceNow instance (any recent release). For the multi-customer rollup, Domain Separation should be enabled, with each customer as a domain.
  • A ServiceNow service account in the global domain with the itil and REST roles. This single account is what the app uses to read across your customer domains and write records back.
  • A monday.com account where you'll add the board view.
Why the global domain? A service account in global can see every customer domain, so one connection covers all your customers — while each customer's data stays isolated in its own domain.

03Install the app

  1. Install ServiceNow MSP Bridge from the monday marketplace.
  2. Open any board → click the + next to the view tabs → Apps → ServiceNow MSP Bridge.
  3. The MSP Command Center loads (showing sample data until you connect).

04Connect ServiceNow

  1. Click Connect ServiceNow in the top bar.
  2. Enter your instance subdomain (e.g. dev12345 for dev12345.service-now.com), the service-account username, and its password.
  3. Click Connect. The credentials are validated and stored encrypted. The rollup fills with your customer domains and live numbers.

Your credentials are encrypted at rest and used only to talk to your own ServiceNow instance.

05Authorize monday

To sync ServiceNow records onto your boards, the app needs permission to create and update items in your monday account. Complete the one-time monday authorization (OAuth) when prompted. This grants the app board and webhook access in your account — each account authorizes its own.

06Using the command center

The rollup

Each row is a customer domain with its open incidents, changes, problems, requests, SLA breaches and MTTR. The tiles at the top total everything across customers.

Drill in & act

  • Click a customer domain to expand its records, grouped by type.
  • Change a record's state or add a work note — it writes straight back to ServiceNow.
  • + New creates a record (incident/change/problem/request) in that customer's domain.

07Sync & two-way

  1. Link a customer domain to the current board (the Sync column) to map where its records sync.
  2. Click Sync now to create/update monday items for that domain's records. Newly-linked domains backfill their existing records automatically.
  3. Click Enable 2-way so edits to those monday items push back into ServiceNow.

08Plans & limits

Each plan sets how many customer domains you can connect at once. The badge in the header shows your plan and usage (e.g. Growth · 8/20 domains). Upgrade to link more.

09Troubleshooting

You seeWhat it means / fix
"sample data — couldn't reach ServiceNow"The instance is unreachable (a dev instance may be asleep) or the credentials are wrong. Wake the instance and reconnect.
Empty rollup after connectingThe service account may not be in the global domain, or your records sit in non-customer domains. Confirm the account is in global with itil + REST.
Sync reports 0 createdLink the domain to a board first. Newly-linked domains backfill on the next Sync now.
"monday not authorized"Complete the monday authorization (step 5) so the app can write to your boards.
