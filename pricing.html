Every plan includes

The tier changes how many customers you connect — never which features you get.

  • Multi-domain command center — incidents, changes, problems & requests per customer
  • SLA breach & MTTR rollup across all connected customers
  • Drill-down into any customer's individual records
  • Write-back — change state, add work notes, create records from monday
  • Bi-directional sync — ServiceNow records ⇄ monday items
  • Four ITSM object types — incident, change, problem, request
  • Per-customer isolation from a single ServiceNow connection
  • Encrypted at rest, verified requests, per-account OAuth

A customer domain is one customer's isolated domain in your domain-separated ServiceNow instance. You're billed on how many you actively connect — not on monday seats or ServiceNow licenses.

All paid plans start with a 14-day free trial. Billing and upgrades are handled through monday.com's secure checkout; prices are in USD, excluding any applicable taxes.
