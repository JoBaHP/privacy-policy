01Who we are & scope

ServiceNow MSP Bridge (the "App") is a monday.com marketplace application provided by [your legal / company name] ("we", "us", "our"). The App connects a customer's ServiceNow instance to their monday.com account so that managed-service-provider (MSP) teams can view and act on ITSM records — across customer domains — inside monday.

This policy applies to the App only. It does not cover monday.com or ServiceNow themselves, each of which is governed by its own privacy policy and terms.

02Data the app accesses

The App accesses only the data needed to bridge your ServiceNow instance and your monday account. It does not use tracking cookies or advertising, and it does not collect personal data beyond what is contained in the records and identifiers below.

03How we use it

We use the data solely to provide the App's features: the per-domain rollup, drill-down, write-back (creating and updating ServiceNow records), and bi-directional sync between your ServiceNow instance and your monday account.

We do not sell or rent your data, use it for advertising, profile you, or share it for anyone else's marketing.

04Storage, security & isolation

  • The App runs on monday Code, monday.com's managed server infrastructure.
  • ServiceNow credentials and monday OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) within monday's secure app storage.
  • All traffic travels over TLS/HTTPS.
  • Every application request is authenticated with a monday-signed session token; unverified requests are rejected.
  • Inbound webhooks are verified before they are processed.
  • Each customer account's connection and data are isolated — one account cannot access another's ServiceNow connection or records.

05Sharing & sub-processors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. It is shared only with the services strictly required to operate the App:

  • monday.com / monday Code — hosts the App and is the platform it runs within. monday's privacy policy applies to data held there.
  • Your own ServiceNow instance — the App reads from and writes to it on your behalf, using the credentials you provide.

No other third parties receive your data. There are no advertising or analytics networks.

06Retention & deletion

Stored credentials and OAuth tokens are retained only while your connection is active. You are in control:

  • Disconnect ServiceNow in the App to delete the stored ServiceNow credentials for your account.
  • Uninstall the App to remove its stored data associated with your account.
  • To request erasure of any remaining data, contact us (below) and we will delete it without undue delay.

07Your rights

Depending on where you are — for example under the GDPR (EEA), UK GDPR, or CCPA (California) — you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing.

Because the App processes ITSM data that you already control in ServiceNow and monday, you can exercise most of these rights directly in those systems. For any data the App itself stores, contact us and we will honor your request.

08International transfers

The App is hosted on monday Code in the United States region. If you use it from another country, your data may be processed in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards.

09Children

The App is a business tool. It is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.
